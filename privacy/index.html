Overview

Moodsk is provided by Ajigu Studio. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Files and icon designs

Moodsk accesses only files and folders that you select or act on through its Finder extension. Image rendering and icon editing happen on your Mac. Ajigu Studio does not receive your file contents, images, or icon designs.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. Moodsk receives product and entitlement status from Apple, but Ajigu Studio does not receive your payment card details. Moodsk may communicate with Apple services to load products and verify purchases.

Local data

App preferences and editing state may be stored locally on your Mac. Removing the app may remove its local data. Icons written to files or folders remain until you change or restore them.

Children and changes

Moodsk is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app changes and will revise the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@ajigu.com.
