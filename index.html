LAYER-BASED EDITOR

How do you design custom folder icons?

Layer images, symbols and text freely. Font families and weights, automatic sizing, outlines and effects — every detail is yours to decide.

  • Live previewThe selected layer is highlighted; border and radius guides appear as you scale.
  • Automatic sizingText adapts to its container, so titles and monograms always fit.
  • Undo / redoBoth edits and layer order can be reverted, so experiment freely.
  • Sharp exportIcons render at 4×, staying crisp in Finder and on the desktop.

Choose an icon, adjust its design, then apply it to your selected files or folders. Moodsk also supports batch replacement, so you can reuse one design across multiple items.

DRAG & DROP

How do you change folder icons in batches?

Drag files or folders into the window and the icon is replaced the moment you let go. You can also export a brand-new folder with its icon already set.

  • Single or multi-file drops, plus the system open panel
  • White outline switch for light wallpapers
  • Old icons are cleared before replacing, so nothing lingers

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which Macs does Moodsk support?

Moodsk runs on macOS 14 or later, including Apple Silicon Macs.

What can I use to create icons?

Import your own images (PNG, JPG, HEIC or SVG), pick from thousands of SF Symbols, or compose icons from text and emoji. Curated icon packs are included.

How do I restore an original icon?

Drop the files back into Moodsk to restore them, or right-click any file in Finder and choose restore with the bundled Finder extension. Batch restore is supported.

Does Moodsk upload my files anywhere?

No. Moodsk renders and applies icons on your Mac without uploading your files or designs. Apple services handle in-app purchases.
